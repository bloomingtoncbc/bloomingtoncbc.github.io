**2024 Bloomington Christmas Bird Count Fun Facts**

- We had 60 field observers across 24 areas, who collectively found 56 species and 7832 total birds.
  - Due to many regular counters being unavailable, and the inclement weather - areas 4, 8, and 19 were not counted. A few other areas only had feeder counters.
  - Though we found 3 more species than last year, we were down almost 4000 individual birds counted.
- Mallards win the highest counted species award this year!
- Liz found a fox sparrow in area 10, and also a Harlan's red tailed hawk.
- Ryan saw red-breasted mergansers in Area 17.
- Winter wrens were seen in areas 7 and 18.
