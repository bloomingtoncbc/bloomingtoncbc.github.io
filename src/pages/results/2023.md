**2023 Bloomington Christmas Bird Count Fun Facts**

- We had 69 field observers and 8 feeder counters across 20 areas who collectively found 53 species and 11,685 individual birds.
- Canada geese seen in every area, for a total of 3141 and by far the most counted of all species.
- Hardly any brown creepers this year! Only 2 total.
- Only one white throated sparrow seen - by Lisa Stanford at her home feeders in Bloomington.
- The Lebanon Hills red-headed woodpecker made an appearance in Area 1.
- Stan reported 2 snow buntings in area 19.
- Three swamp sparrows were found, each in a different area:
  - Melia in Area 3.
  - Steve, Becca and Katie in Area 7.
  - Jeanne and Elizabeth in Area 18.
- Monica and the Urban Bird Collective found a red shouldered hawk in Area 5.
- Jim and Maureen found a Wilson's snipe (got a photo of it!) in Area 16.

Thank you to everyone who participated!
