title: Submitting Results
---

- Count results should be submitted to Liz via [email](mailto:bloomingtonmncbc@gmail.com) (take a photo or scan.)
  - Due to the large number of counters, to facilitate data entry, results must be submitted using [the current year count sheet](/count-info/count-sheet/).
    - Do not use a copy of the count sheet from prior years, as the species list may have changed.
  - Make sure to write legibly and fill out all the information at the top of the form.
  - Use dark ink, and good lighting if taking a photo of the page.
  - Each species counted should have a **single number** next to it (no math or dots, etc.)
  - Please submit results as soon as possible after the count.
    - Per Audubon CBC protocol only count results from our designated day and within the count circle will be included in the tally.
  - Also please be responsive to emails about your submitted results, if there are any questions, or we need to confirm your data.
- Results submitted otherwise will be returned with a request to follow the above instructions. I appreciate your cooperation!
