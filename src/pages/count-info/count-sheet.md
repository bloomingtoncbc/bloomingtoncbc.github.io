This is the official count sheet for the Bloomington CBC Circle. It reflects species that we expect to see (based on analysis of past counts, see method below), which don't require additional documentation. Anything that is not listed on the sheet must be documented. Results will only be accepted using this form. Please ensure that you are entering the total number for each species in numeric form (not dots or multiple numbers.)

You can [download the form](https://drive.google.com/file/d/10syowmyMib2zDo_UkaKqWB8-kV2h3D77/view?usp=drive_link) and print out to use during the count. Keep in mind that this is the required form to submit results.

The method and criteria used to create this form:

- Source data from [Bloomington CBC data 1966-2025](https://moumn.org/CBC/birds_table.php?cid=157)
- Used a 20 year window to confirm that all 75 species from the 2025 form still qualify (with a minimum of 2 sightings.) None were dropped as of yet.
  - 31 species were marked bold, being counted each of the last 10 years.
  - 21 species were marked \* due to only being counted <= 3 of the last 10 years.
  - 1 species (Common Grackle) marked \*\*, 0 counted in the last 10 years.
  - New addition: Hermit Thrush seen in 5 of the last 10 years.
  - Other species not added, years counted in the last 10:
    - Swamp Sparrow - 4/10
    - Winter Wren - 3/10
    - American Goshawk, Tufted Titmouse, Yellow-rumped Warbler, Red-headed Woodpecker, White-winged crossbill - 2/10
